## What is Nordath?

Nordath is a business intelligence and data automation platform built for the modern real estate ecosystem. We transform complex geographic, cadastral and corporate registry datasets into intuitive dashboards, automated prospecting workflows and actionable land sourcing insights.

By eliminating the manual friction of parcel identification and feasibility analysis, Nordath enables land developers, investors and property professionals to pinpoint high-potential opportunities faster, qualify plots with precision and scale their acquisition pipelines.
